# FarmFriend AI — Known Limitations & Responsible AI Scope

**Document Version**: 2.0  
**Updated**: 2026-08-13  

---

## 1. Responsible AI Guidance & Non-Claims

1. **Decision Support, Not Guaranteed Yield**:
   FarmFriend AI generates suitability estimates and crop rankings to assist agricultural decision-making. Recommendations do not guarantee crop yields, pest immunity, or financial outcomes.

2. **No Profit or Financial Projections**:
   The engine explicitly excludes market prices, crop futures, and profit calculations to prevent misleading financial speculation.

3. **Local KVK Validation Recommended**:
   Farmers are encouraged to cross-reference recommendations with their local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or District Agricultural Officer.

---

## 2. Technical & Data Limitations

1. **Micro-Level Soil Heterogeneity**:
   Soil properties can vary within a single farm plot. Soil Health Card testing at specific sampling points represents localized averages.

2. **Uncertainty under Extreme Climate Outliers**:
   Inputs exceeding historical meteorological distributions (e.g. seasonal rainfall > 3000 mm or temperature > 48°C) trigger out-of-distribution warnings with reduced data confidence.

3. **Perennial vs Seasonal Management**:
   Perennial orchard crops (e.g. Grapes, Pomegranate) require permanent drip irrigation infrastructure and multi-year capital investment. Selecting "Kharif Rainfed" automatically prioritizes seasonal field crops.
