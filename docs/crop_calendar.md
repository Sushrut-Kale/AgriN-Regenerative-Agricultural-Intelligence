## 1. Seasonal Field Crops vs Perennial Horticulture

Agricultural decision-support systems must clearly distinguish between **Seasonal Field Crops** (sown and harvested within 3–8 months of a specific season) and **Perennial / Horticultural Crops** (multi-year trees and vines requiring permanent land commitment and long-term drip irrigation infrastructure).

### Crop Categories & Classifications

```
                       CROP CATALOGUE
                             │
         ┌───────────────────┴───────────────────┐
         ▼                                       ▼
  SEASONAL FIELD CROPS                    PERENNIAL HORTICULTURE
  (Kharif / Rabi / Zaid)                  (Multi-year Vines & Orchards)
  • Cotton (6-8 months)                   • Grapes (15-20 yrs, high drip req)
  • Soybean (3-4 months)                  • Pomegranate (10-15 yrs, drip req)
  • Pigeonpea / Tur (6-7 months)          • Mango (20+ yrs, orchard)
  • Chickpea / Gram (4 months)            • Banana (12-15 months, heavy water)
  • Bajra / Jowar / Maize (3-4 months)    • Citrus / Orange (15+ yrs)
  • Mung / Urad (2.5-3 months)            • Coconut (30+ yrs)
```
