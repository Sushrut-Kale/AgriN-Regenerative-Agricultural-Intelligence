## Verified Agricultural Data & Knowledge Sources

### 1. Government of India Soil Health Card Portal
- **URL**: [https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/](https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/)
- **Organization**: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, GoI
- **Usage**: Canonical definitions, standard testing units, and rating scales for 12 Soil Health Card parameters (`N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B, pH, EC, OC`).

### 2. ICRISAT District Level Database
- **URL**: [https://data.icrisat.org/district-level-data/](https://data.icrisat.org/district-level-data/)
- **Organization**: International Crops Research Institute for the Semi-Arid Tropics (ICRISAT)
- **Usage**: District-level meso agricultural data across India, including crop area, production, rainfall, and agro-climatic zone classifications.

### 3. Government of India Open Data Portal (Data.gov.in)
- **URL**: [https://data.gov.in/sector/agriculture](https://data.gov.in/sector/agriculture)
- **Organization**: National Informatics Centre (NIC), Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology, GoI
- **Usage**: Open government datasets covering district-wise crop yields, seasonal rainfall, and irrigation distribution.

### 4. ICAR-NBSS&LUP (National Bureau of Soil Survey & Land Use Planning)
- **URL**: [https://nbsslup.icar.gov.in/](https://nbsslup.icar.gov.in/)
- **Organization**: Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR)
- **Usage**: Soil series taxonomies, Vertisol/black soil properties in Marathwada/Beed/Parbhani, and crop land suitability criteria.

### 5. FAOSTAT Database
- **URL**: [https://www.fao.org/faostat/](https://www.fao.org/faostat/)
- **Organization**: Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO)
- **Usage**: Supplementary international crop physiological thresholds and ecological growing requirements.
